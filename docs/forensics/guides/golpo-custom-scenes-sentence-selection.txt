How to Map Sentences to Scenes in Golpo Before Video Production

Golpo can now show you how your script will be divided into scenes before production starts . With Custom scenes and sentence selection , you begin with Golpo's automatic scene map and then decide which sentences should stay together, where a new scene should begin, and how many scenes the final video should use.

This gives you control over the video's visual rhythm before the illustrations, narration timing, and transitions are generated. Instead of discovering an awkward scene break after the full video is finished, you can fix the structure first.

In the example below: Golpo allowed up to 14 scenes for a 43-sentence script. The map was reorganized into 12 scenes, saved, and then used to produce the final video.

Why sentence-to-scene mapping matters

A scene is more than a container for words. It controls which part of the narration shares one visual idea. A useful scene map helps the video feel intentional:

Give an important line its own scene when it needs emphasis or a visual reveal.

Combine short, closely related sentences when they should play as one smooth moment.

Split a crowded scene when it contains too many ideas for one visual composition.

Keep setup and payoff together when separating them would make the story harder to follow.

Improve transitions and subtitle timing by placing scene boundaries at natural changes in thought.

The feature does not make you plan every scene from scratch. Golpo creates the first map. You only reshape the parts that need a different pace, grouping, or emphasis.

How to customize scenes before production

Add your script and choose the main video settings. Paste or write the script, then select the video type, duration, voice, orientation, language, style, pacing, and any other options you want.

Enable “Custom scenes and sentence selection.” The option appears with an Edit sentences button and the current total scene count.

Select “Edit sentences.” Golpo opens the Build your scene map screen and displays its automatic scene suggestion.

Review the proposed structure. Use the numbered scene tabs and scene cards to check each sentence range, estimated scene duration, sentence count, and word count. Golpo also displays the maximum number of scenes supported for that script and engine.

Change the total scene count if needed. Use the minus and plus controls. You can reorganize the map within the engine maximum shown on screen. In this example, Golpo's maximum was 14 scenes and the final map used 12.

Move sentence boundaries. Drag the divider between sentence rows, drag an individual sentence into another scene, or use First sentence and Last sentence on a scene card to shift its boundary.

Split or combine scenes. Use Split scene , Combine prev. , or Combine next. The screen also provides Auto-balance , Undo , and Reset .

Check the map before saving. Confirm that every sentence is assigned. Review any Long scene labels and make sure each scene contains one coherent visual beat.

Select “Save scene map.” Back on the main settings screen, confirm the revised number beside Total scenes .

Continue to production. Select Continue with 12 Scenes —or the count shown for your map—and complete the remaining confirmation steps.

How to review the saved scene map later

The scene map remains visible after production:

Open your Library .

Open the settings for the relevant video card.

Select View Configuration .

Find Custom Scene Selection . The row displays the saved count, such as 12 scenes configured .

Expand the row to review every scene's sentence range and its starting and ending sentence.

In the Library, open the video's settings and choose View Configuration. Custom Scene Selection shows “12 scenes configured”; expand it to inspect the stored sentence ranges. 

This makes the finished video's structure easy to audit. You can confirm that production used the intended 12-scene map without reconstructing the decisions from the final playback.

Practical scene-mapping tips

Read for meaning, not equal length. Two scenes do not need the same number of sentences if one idea deserves more time.

Use a separate scene for a reveal. In the example, “The Zodiac.” begins its own scene so the name lands with emphasis.

Watch the estimated duration. A long scene can work, but it should contain one coherent idea that can support a sustained visual.

Keep connected details together. Group context, action, and consequence when one visual can explain them more clearly than several rapid cuts.

Check every boundary once. Read the last sentence of one scene and the first sentence of the next. The change should feel deliberate.

Use Undo before Reset. Undo is useful for a single unwanted move; Reset returns to the original suggested map.

What does Custom scenes and sentence selection change?

It changes which existing script sentences belong to each scene and how many scenes the video uses before production. The scene-map screen does not rewrite the sentences.

Do I have to build every scene manually?

No. Golpo creates an automatic scene map first. You can keep it, use Auto-balance , or change only the boundaries that need attention in the embedded walkthrough .

Can I choose any number of scenes?

You can change the count within the limit shown in the scene-map screen. Golpo displays the engine maximum. In the worked example , that maximum was 14 and the saved map used 12 scenes.

Can I move one sentence without rebuilding the map?

Yes. Drag an individual sentence into another scene, drag the divider between sentence rows, or use the boundary controls on the selected scene card. The first embedded MP4 shows these controls in use.

Can I undo changes?

Yes. The scene-map screen includes Undo and Reset , as well as Auto-balance .

Where can I see the saved scene map after the video is created?

Open the video's card in the Library, open its settings, choose View Configuration , and expand Custom Scene Selection . See the configuration screenshot and exact steps .
