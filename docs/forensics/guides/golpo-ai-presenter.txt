AI Presenter: upload a photo and present the video

AI Presenter is the path for putting yourself, a teammate, or another authorized person on screen. Upload one clear portrait, review how it sits in the frame, and save it. The next video can use that same presenter again.

With voice cloning , the presenter can speak in your own voice and tone. A built-in voice works too, and it is a separate choice from the photo.

1. Upload and frame a clear portrait

Open Talking AI Avatars → Create Avatar . Drag in a PNG, JPEG, or WebP up to 10 MB , or click to browse.

Use one person, facing the camera, with the full head, chin, and shoulders visible. Even lighting, a simple background, and a sharp image give the animation a clean start. Group photos, hands over the face, dark sunglasses, heavy shadows, blur, and cropped hair make a weaker presenter.

Frame the portrait, then choose Continue to review . The creation screen says your portrait and saved presenter stay in your personal library by default.

Start an AI Presenter with one clear portrait. 

2. Choose Natural or Polished

Natural uses your original portrait and the framing you approved. It is ready immediately, with no creation credit charge .

Polished creates an AI-styled portrait designed to keep you recognizable. It costs 1 credit after successful creation .

3. Approve layouts, name it, and save

Preview Side by side , Top and bottom , Round overlay , and Full screen . Approve the layouts that look right, enter a name, confirm image use, and save. The presenter then lives in My Avatars .

Review the style and layouts, then save the AI Presenter for reuse.

On a landscape split, the presenter can take 25%, 30%, 35%, 40%, or 50% of the width, on the left or the right . A 50/50 split gives the face and the drawing equal room. A 35% presenter leaves more space for diagrams and labels.

Auto uses the approved layouts that fit each scene. When at least two visible layouts are compatible, it uses at least two. It may briefly hide the presenter so an important drawing stays readable. The preview tabs let you inspect Side by side, Round overlay, and Full screen. Switching those tabs still leaves generation set to Auto.

Four ways to use a presenter

The useful pattern is the same in each case. One approved explanation. One recognizable presenter. Visuals that carry the idea. A person still handles the judgment, the questions, and the parts that depend on the individual in front of them.

1. Founder updates, with your own AI Presenter

A release update has a simple job: what changed, why it matters, and what to do next. An AI Presenter lets an authorized founder deliver that update from a saved portrait, so the next release does not need another recording session. Each update still needs an approved script, a voice choice, accurate product visuals, and a final review.

In this example, Shraman's Natural AI Presenter walks through the portrait-or-Golpo-Avatar choice, separate narration, layouts, and the real credit rates. It is a release-style explanation you can adapt for your own product news.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between AI Presenter and Golpo Avatars?

AI Presenter is a presenter you create from a portrait or selfie. Golpo Avatars are 20 ready-made presenters.

Do I need my own photo?

Only for an AI Presenter. For a Golpo Avatar, open the library and select a presenter. No upload required.

What is the difference between Natural and Polished?

Natural keeps your original portrait and approved framing, with no creation credit charge. Polished makes an AI-styled portrait designed to stay recognizable, and it uses 1 credit after successful creation.

Does the photo clone my voice?

The photo sets appearance. Choose the narration voice on its own. The Golpo Avatar examples use built-in voices. To have an AI Presenter speak in your tone, follow how to clone your voice for AI video .

Can I reuse an AI Presenter?

Yes. Save it in My Avatars and select it on the next video.

Can I make vertical videos?

Yes. Use Top and bottom for a portrait 9:16 frame, or Auto when you want Golpo to choose. The layout guide includes vertical 50/50, vertical 35%, and vertical Auto examples.

Will the presenter stay on screen for the whole Auto video?

Auto may hide the presenter briefly so important content stays unobstructed. It chooses among the layouts you approved that fit the scene.

Which plans include this?

AI Presenter is included on every Golpo plan . Free, Starter, and the API plan are the exceptions. API access is coming soon.
