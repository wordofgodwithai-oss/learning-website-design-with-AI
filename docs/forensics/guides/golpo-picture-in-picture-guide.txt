Picture in Picture lets you keep a real presenter visible while Golpo creates the animated explanation around their narration. Record with your webcam inside Golpo or upload an existing video of somebody speaking, then place that presenter in a corner bubble, beside the animation, above it, or below it.

The result combines the credibility and personality of a face-to-camera recording with the clarity of a visual explainer.

What is Picture in Picture in Golpo?

Picture in Picture is an Own Narration feature that uses a video recording for two jobs at once:

Plan availability: Own Narration, including Picture in Picture, is available on the Business plan and all higher tiers . See the full Golpo pricing breakdown for the current plan comparison.

The recording's audio becomes the narration that drives the timing of the Golpo animation.

The recording's camera footage remains visible as the presenter layer.

Golpo removes the uploaded video's picture only when you select Audio in Video. That choice uses the recording's audio track as narration while Golpo generates new visuals. When you select Picture in Picture , Golpo preserves the presenter video and displays it in your chosen overlay or split-screen layout.

You can supply the presenter in either of two ways:

Record Webcam: record camera and microphone live without leaving Golpo.

Upload Audio or Video: upload an existing video of yourself or another authorized speaker.

How to create a Picture-in-Picture video

Step 1: Turn on Own Narration

Open the video creation screen and switch on the Own Narration toggle at the top. This changes the workflow from an AI-selected voice to narration supplied by you.

Step 2: Record with your webcam or upload a speaking video

Choose Record Webcam to capture camera and audio live. Golpo shows a countdown, recording timer, and stop control. If the recording already exists, choose the upload option and add the video file instead.

Use clear speech and keep the recording close to the desired final video length. Golpo synchronizes the animation to this narration.

Step 3: Choose Picture in Picture

Once the video is attached, find Video usage and select Picture in Picture . Do not leave Audio in Video selected if you want the speaker to remain visible.

Step 4: Choose Round Overlay or Split screen

Select Round Overlay for a compact circular presenter bubble. Then choose the corner: Left Top , Right Top , Left Bottom , or Right Bottom . The preview shows the placement before you generate.

After turning on Own Narration and adding the video, select Picture in Picture, Round Overlay, and a corner position. 

Select Split screen when the presenter should have a dedicated panel. Use it to place the person to the left or right of the content, or above or below it when that arrangement fits the orientation and composition of the video.

Step 5: Tune the split-screen composition

For split screen, adjust the controls until the preview gives both the presenter and the explanation enough room:

Webcam width: choose 25%, 30%, 35%, 40%, or 50%.

Webcam fill: choose Crop to fit for an edge-to-edge panel or Full video to preserve the complete camera frame.

Framing: pan the crop left or right so the speaker remains centered.

Side: place the webcam panel on the left or right. For vertical or stacked compositions, use the available top or bottom arrangement.

Split-screen controls let you choose panel width, crop behavior, framing, and side while watching the preview. 

Step 6: Confirm that you have permission

Check the certification box to confirm that you own the recording or have explicit permission to use its video and audio. This applies whether you recorded yourself, recorded another person, or uploaded an existing file.

Step 7: Choose the animation engine and generate

Select Golpo Canvas or Golpo Sketch , finish the remaining style, language, pacing, and orientation settings, and generate the video. Golpo uses the narration for timing, creates the explanatory scenes, and composites the presenter in the layout you selected.

A practical rule: use a round overlay when the presenter's face adds warmth and trust, and use split screen when the presenter's gestures, expression, or direct address carry substantial meaning.

Where Picture in Picture is useful

YouTubers and creators

Explain a product, break down a news story, teach a technique, react to a concept, or turn a simple talking-head recording into a more visual episode. The presenter keeps the channel's personality while Golpo adds diagrams and scene changes.

Teachers and course creators

Record a lesson, worked example, revision session, or course introduction. Students can see the teacher while the concepts appear as drawings, timelines, examples, and supporting scenes.

CEOs and company leaders

Deliver a company-wide announcement, quarterly update, strategic narrative, culture message, or change-management communication. The leader stays visible while the animation clarifies priorities, milestones, and decisions.

CFOs and finance teams

Present financial results to a board, regulator, authority, investor group, or internal leadership team. The speaker can introduce the numbers while Golpo visualizes trends, drivers, risks, and next steps.

Marketers and growth teams

Send personalized outreach with a human introduction and a tailored explainer beside it. Use the same approach for campaign recaps, customer stories, event invitations, launch announcements, and account-based marketing.

Sales teams

Record a personal opening for a proposal, product walkthrough, or follow-up. The seller remains on camera while the explanation highlights the buyer's problem, the proposed solution, and the call to action.

Product managers and founders

Walk through a product decision, roadmap, launch, or feature concept. Use the animation to explain the system while the presenter supplies context and conviction.

Learning and development teams

Turn a trainer's recording into onboarding, compliance, process, or skills content. The trainer keeps a human presence while the scenes make steps and policies easier to follow.

Consultants, coaches, and experts

Deliver frameworks, assessments, recommendations, and personalized advice with a more engaging visual layer than a webcam recording alone.

Customer success and support

Create welcome messages, implementation guidance, troubleshooting walkthroughs, and renewal reviews. A recognizable person can build trust while Golpo handles the explanation.

Best practices for a polished result

Prioritize clear audio. The narration drives the timing, so use a quiet room and keep the microphone close.

Frame for the layout. Leave headroom and avoid sitting too close to an edge. Split-screen crops need room to pan.

Look toward the content. If you know the presenter will sit on the left, a slight eye-line toward the right can make the composition feel intentional.

Use even front lighting. A clearly lit face stays readable in both narrow panels and small round overlays.

Choose the smallest useful presenter size. Start around 25% to 35% for split screen, then increase only when facial expression or gestures need more emphasis.

Preview the crop. Use the framing control to keep eyes and face centered before generation.

Protect important visual areas. Do not place a round overlay over a corner that needs labels, equations, or diagrams.

Match the orientation to the destination. Use portrait for vertical feeds and landscape for presentations, YouTube, courses, and company communication.

Keep the delivery natural. Speak in complete thoughts, pause between major ideas, and avoid rushing through transitions.

Use only authorized recordings. Record yourself or use video and audio for which you have explicit rights and consent.
