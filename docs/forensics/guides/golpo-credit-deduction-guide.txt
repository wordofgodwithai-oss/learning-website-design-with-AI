How Credits Work on Golpo AI: Deduction Rules Explained

Golpo AI uses a credit system, and the core rule is simple: 1 credit = 60 seconds of Golpo Sketch or Golpo Canvas video, or 30 seconds of Golpo Studio video . Everything else is just how that time is measured and when the charge happens.

Golpo Avatar videos use 3 credits per minute of finished video, while Own Avatar videos (using an avatar you create from an image) use 5 credits per minute of finished video.

There are four scenarios where credits are deducted — prompt/script generation, pre-render script review, own narration uploads, and editing. Each one works a little differently. Here's exactly how.

1. Prompt or Script Mode

When you generate a video using a prompt or a script, you choose a target duration before generating . Golpo deducts credits based on that chosen duration — not the exact final runtime.

You select 4 minutes → 4 credits for Golpo Sketch or Golpo Canvas, or 8 credits for Golpo Studio

Final video is 3:56 or 4:03 → doesn't matter, you were charged for the 4-minute target

The benefit here is predictability . You know the cost upfront before the video is even generated.

2. “Edit Script Before Creating Video”

When Edit script before creating video is checked, Golpo deducts the selected duration’s credits in two stages:

10% is deducted after the script is created 

The remaining 90% is deducted after the video is created 

For a 4-minute Golpo Sketch or Golpo Canvas video, 0.4 credits are deducted after the script is created , then 3.6 credits after the video is created : 4 credits total.

3. Own Narration Mode

This works differently because you're uploading your own audio or video file. In this case, credits are deducted based on the actual length of what you upload .

Your narration is 2 minutes 10 seconds → the generated video will be that same length

Credits are deducted based on that real duration 

There's no target duration selector here. The length of your upload determines both the video length and the credit cost.

4. Editing — The One That Causes Confusion

This applies to all paid plans :

The first edited version is free across sessions until finalized.

Later edited versions cost credits equal to the full video length 

You can edit as many frames as you want in that same version, across sessions — no extra credits

If you finalize a new version later, the full-length credit cost applies again

So if you have a 4-minute video, its first edited version is free. Edit ten more frames in that same version — still free. After finalizing, a second edited version costs 4 credits.

Quick Summary

Prompt/Script mode: Credits based on the duration you select before generating

Edit script before creating video: 10% is deducted after script creation and the remaining 90% after video creation; the total stays equal to the selected duration

Own Narration mode: Credits based on the actual length of your uploaded audio/video

Editing (all paid plans): First edited version free until finalized, across sessions; later versions charged at full video length
