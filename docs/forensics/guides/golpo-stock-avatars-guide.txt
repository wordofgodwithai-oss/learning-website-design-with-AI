A presenter can make an explanation feel personal. With Golpo Stock Avatars , you can add that presenter without uploading a selfie or creating a custom avatar. Choose one of Golpo's 20 ready-to-use presenters, select a narration voice, and combine the avatar with the visuals that explain your idea.

Each example below pairs a named stock presenter with a distinct video, voice, and visual style. You can play the videos inside this guide and use the player's full-screen button to expand them.

Combine a ready-to-use presenter with Golpo, the world's best AI explainer video maker, for a winning combination: a face that guides the audience and animated visuals that make the explanation easier to follow.

Meet Golpo's ready-to-use presenters

Open Talking AI Avatars → Golpo Avatars to browse the stock library. Each card displays a presenter name and a short description, such as Maya — warm educator , Arjun — confident business presenter , Sofia — friendly product guide , or Daniel — calm technical host .

The library screenshot shows 20 avatars ready for video generation. You can choose a stock presenter directly; you do not need to upload an image or go through the custom-avatar creation workflow.

Start in the Golpo Avatars tab to choose a ready-to-use presenter. 

Browse more presenters and choose a tone that suits your lesson, update, or explanation. 

How to use a Stock Avatar, step by step

1. Start with the idea or script

Open Golpo and begin your video. Add a prompt describing what you want to explain, or supply your own script when you want control over the narration.

For a first attempt, choose one clear idea. The gallery gives you practical starting points: Maya introduces an empathy story, Daniel explains how an internet click travels, and Mateo explains why Earth has seasons.

2. Choose the video orientation

The 20 stock examples in this guide use Horizontal . Choose that orientation to follow these examples. If you are making a vertical video, choose the portrait format and review how the presenter and visuals share that frame.

3. Enable Talking AI Avatar

In the video settings, turn on Talking AI Avatar . Open the avatar chooser using Golpo / Golpo Avatars .

4. Select your stock presenter by name

Browse the ready-to-use avatars and choose a presenter. Use the name in the gallery to find the same avatar in Golpo: select Maya for The Invisible Backpack We All Carry , Sofia for The Button Nobody Wanted to Click , or Lukas for Which Decisions Deserve More Analysis .

Check that the selected card shows the presenter you intended to use.

5. Choose the narration voice

Select the voice separately from the avatar. The examples below use built-in voices , with no voice cloning. Each video card lists the voice used, such as Female 3 , Female 4 , Male 3 , or Male 4 .

For example, Maya's video uses Female 3 , while Arjun's uses Male 4 . Choose those voices to follow the example setup, or choose a different voice for your own narration.

6. Choose how the presenter appears

Choose Side by side , Round overlay , Full screen , or Auto , depending on your video and orientation. Check the live preview to see the balance between the presenter and the supporting content.

All 20 stock videos in this gallery use Auto . Auto chooses among compatible approved layouts for each scene. It can change the presentation and may briefly hide the presenter to keep important content unobstructed.

7. Select the visual engine and style

Choose Golpo Canvas or Golpo Sketch , then choose a visual style. The presenter stays part of the explanation while the surrounding visuals take on that treatment.

Maya's empathy story uses Canvas · Illustration · Expanded . Daniel's internet explainer uses Sketch · Infographics . Minh's startup story uses Canvas · Notebook · Compact . For Canvas, choose the Compact or Expanded variant shown beside the video. Sketch examples show N/A for Variant. Every configuration panel lists the corresponding engine, style, and variant so you can try the same combination.

8. Set optional background music

Choose music that suits the explanation. The examples use options such as Inspirational, Engaging, Documentary, Jazz, Lo-fi, Hyper, and Dramatic. Each card lists its music choice; use it as a starting point when recreating that example's setup.

9. Review the settings

Confirm the presenter name, voice, orientation, layout, style, and video duration.

10. Generate and review the video

Continue with video generation. Review the finished video in your Library and check the narration, presenter, visual clarity, and pacing. Use the examples below to compare how the same Stock Avatar feature works across different subjects and styles.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a selfie to use a Stock Avatar?

No. Open Golpo Avatars and select a ready-to-use presenter from the library.

How many Stock Avatars can I choose from?

The library shown here contains 20 stock presenters. This guide includes a corresponding example for each of them.

Does choosing a Stock Avatar automatically choose a voice?

Choose and review the narration voice separately. Each example lists the built-in voice used so you can follow its setup. These videos do not use a cloned voice.

Can I combine a Stock Avatar with Canvas or Sketch?

Yes. The gallery includes ten Canvas examples and ten Sketch examples, with a different visual treatment for each presenter.

Can I change the presentation layout?

Yes. Choose a compatible presentation option and inspect the live preview. The examples here use Auto, Horizontal ; the custom Avatar guide also demonstrates split, round overlay, and vertical layouts.

Can I watch and expand the examples without leaving this guide?

Yes. Each example has an embedded YouTube player. Press Play, then use the full-screen button to expand the video.
