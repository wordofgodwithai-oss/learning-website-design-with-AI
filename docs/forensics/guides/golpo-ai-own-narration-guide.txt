Golpo AI generates narration for your videos automatically — but sometimes you want your own voice, your own recording, or audio you have already produced. Maybe you recorded a voiceover using ElevenLabs. Maybe you have a Zoom recording of a presentation you want turned into a polished explainer. Maybe you just want to talk into your microphone and let Golpo handle the visuals.

The Own Narration toggle makes all of this possible. When you turn it on, Golpo stops generating narration and instead uses whatever audio you provide — then builds the video around it. The AI listens to your audio and generates visuals that match what you are saying.

Plan availability: On subscription plans, Own Narration is available on the Business, Scale, and Enterprise tiers. See the live Golpo pricing page for the latest plan details.

This guide walks through every way to use Own Narration, with real examples of what the output looks like.

Where to Find the Own Narration Toggle

When you open the Golpo AI video creation page, you will see the default view with a text prompt area where you describe your video topic. In the top-right area, there are two toggles: Own Narration and Script Mode .

The current video creation page, with Own Narration available above the prompt area. 

By default, Own Narration is turned off. In this mode, you write a prompt, Golpo generates a script and narration for you, and then produces the video. This is the standard workflow.

When you flip the Own Narration toggle to ON, the interface changes completely. The text prompt area disappears and is replaced with audio and video input options.

Own Narration with an uploaded video and Picture in Picture selected. 

The interface provides three input controls that cover four common workflows:

Upload Audio or Video — upload an existing audio file or an existing video file

Record Audio — use your microphone to record narration directly in the browser

Record Webcam — capture a new camera-and-audio recording in the browser

Let us go through each one.

Option 1: Upload Your Own Audio File

This is the most common use case. You already have an audio file — maybe you recorded a voiceover, generated speech using a tool like ElevenLabs or other text-to-speech services, or extracted audio from a podcast or presentation.

Here is how it works:

Toggle Own Narration to ON

Click the upload area or drag your audio file into it

Golpo accepts MP3, WAV, M4A, and other common audio formats

Select your video style — Golpo Canvas or Golpo Sketch

Optionally add video instructions (for example, "show more graphs and charts" or "use cinematic transitions")

Hit generate

Golpo transcribes your audio, understands the content, and generates visuals that match your narration. Golpo uses the supplied audio as the narration instead of generating an AI voice.

When to Use Audio Upload

AI-generated voiceovers — use ElevenLabs, Amazon Polly, Google Cloud TTS, or any text-to-speech tool to create a polished voiceover in any voice or language, then upload it to Golpo

Podcast repurposing — take a segment from your podcast and turn it into a visual explainer your audience can share

Multilingual content — record narration in any language and Golpo generates visuals to match

Consistent brand voice — if your company has a specific narrator or voice talent, use their recordings across all your videos

Option 2: Upload an Existing Video

The upload area accepts common video formats too, including MP4 and MOV. After uploading a video, choose how Golpo should use it:

Audio in Video — Golpo uses the video's audio track as narration and generates new visuals around it. The source video's picture is not shown.

Picture in Picture — Golpo keeps the speaker visible alongside the generated content. Choose a round corner overlay or a split-screen layout. See the complete Picture-in-Picture guide for layouts and examples.

The uploaded video's picture is removed only when you select Audio in Video. If you select Picture in Picture , Golpo preserves the presenter video and displays it in your chosen overlay or split-screen layout.

This is incredibly useful for repurposing content you already have:

Zoom recordings — turn a presentation or training session into a polished explainer, with or without the speaker visible

Old marketing videos — retain useful narration while generating an updated visual treatment

Conference talks — turn an executive keynote into a shareable animated explainer

Internal training recordings — combine a presenter with generated teaching visuals

Toggle Own Narration on, upload the video, choose Audio in Video or Picture in Picture , configure the visual settings, certify that you have the right to use the recording, and generate the video.

Option 3: Record Audio Directly

If you do not have a pre-recorded file, you can record your narration directly in Golpo using the Record Audio button. This uses your computer or phone's microphone.

Click Record Audio, speak your narration, stop the recording, and Golpo uses it immediately. No need to save a file, transfer it to your computer, or deal with audio editing software.

This is perfect for:

Quick explainers where you just want to talk through a topic naturally

Internal videos where production polish on the audio is not critical

Iterating fast — record, generate, review, re-record if needed

Option 4: Record With Your Webcam

The Record Webcam option captures both your camera feed and audio. Choose Picture in Picture if you want the recording to remain visible beside the Golpo-generated visuals. Choose Audio in Video if you want Golpo to use only the recording's audio.

Settings You Can Customize

After providing your narration through any of the four methods, you still have control over several settings that affect the final video:

Animation engine — choose Golpo Canvas or Golpo Sketch and use the available Preview links to compare them

Music — add background music or keep it at None to let your narration stand alone

Orientation — choose Horizontal or Vertical when that option is available for your selected configuration

Style and pacing — select from the visual styles and pacing options available for your chosen engine and plan

Video instructions — direct the visual treatment with requests such as “show more graphs and charts”

Video usage and layout — for a video source, choose Audio in Video or Picture in Picture; Picture in Picture adds overlay or split-screen controls

Important: In Own Narration mode, the uploaded or recorded narration determines the video duration, so the normal duration selector is disabled. The recording also supplies the spoken language; changing a language setting does not translate the supplied audio. AI voice selection and Voice Instructions do not apply because Golpo is using your narration.

Before generation, you must certify that you own the uploaded audio or video, or have explicit permission to use it, and that your use complies with applicable laws and third-party rights.

Own Narration vs. Default AI Narration: When to Use Which

Golpo's built-in AI narration is excellent for most use cases. You write a prompt, and it generates a complete video with script and voiceover. Use Own Narration when:

You need a specific voice — your own, your CEO's, or a branded AI voice from ElevenLabs

You already have content recorded — a Zoom call, a podcast segment, a conference talk

You want precise control over what is said — writing a prompt gives you approximate control, but recording your own audio gives you exact control over every word

You are repurposing existing video — the narration is good, but you want fresh, professional visuals

You want your face in the video — webcam recording is only available through Own Narration

For everything else — when you want to describe a topic and let Golpo handle both the script and the visuals — the default prompt-based workflow is faster and requires less preparation.

Getting Started

Go to video.golpoai.com , open the video creation page, and turn on Own Narration . Upload an audio or video file, or record with your microphone or webcam. Then choose your visual settings, confirm you have the required rights to the recording, and generate the video.
