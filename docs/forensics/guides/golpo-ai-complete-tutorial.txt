What Golpo AI Does
Golpo is an AI video generator focused on explainer and whiteboard-style videos. You give it a topic — either by typing a prompt, uploading a document, recording your voice, or providing an existing audio or video file — and it produces a narrated, animated video.
The output is not a slideshow or a template you fill in. Golpo generates original hand-drawn illustrations, syncs them to narration, and animates them with a pen-drawing effect. The result looks like what a professional whiteboard animation studio would produce, but it takes minutes instead of weeks.
Common use cases include:
YouTube educational content and explainers
Corporate training and employee onboarding videos
Product demos and marketing videos
Sales enablement and personalized prospect videos
Course content for educators and coaches
Social media content in vertical or horizontal formats

Golpo supports Google or Microsoft login. Please go to https://video.golpoai.com and click on "Getting Started" on the top or "Start for Free" as shown in this screenshot.

The Create Video Page
When you log in to Golpo and click Create New Video , you land on the video creation page. This is where everything happens. The page is organized from top to bottom: input area at the top, video style selection in the middle, and detailed settings at the bottom.
Let us go through every element on this page.

The Top Bar
Across the top of the page you will see:
Credits — your remaining video credits, shown as a number (for example, 117.562). If you are on a team plan, it says "team credits" next to it. For video generation, 1 credit is 60 seconds of Golpo Canvas/Sketch videos and 30 seconds of Golpo Studio videos
Plan — your current plan name (Free, Starter, Creator, Growth, Business, or Scale), displayed as a clickable link
Voice Clone — a button that takes you to the voice cloning feature (Business and Scale plans)
Library — opens your video library where all generated videos are stored
Upgrade — takes you to the pricing page
Profile icon — click to see your name, email, and a dropdown menu with Add-ons, Edit Video, Team, Manage Billing, and Sign Out
On the left side of the top bar, there is an Instructions dropdown. This lets you save and load reusable instruction presets — handy if you frequently create videos with the same tone, style, or specific guidelines.
Prompt Content: The Main Input Area
The large text box labeled Prompt Content is where you describe your video. This is the primary way to tell Golpo what to make.
You can type anything from a single sentence to a detailed multi-paragraph description. The placeholder text reads: "Describe your video/podcast topic, perspective, and instructions in any language..." 
Good prompts include:
The topic you want explained
The perspective or audience (for example, "explain this to a five-year-old" or "this is for senior software engineers")
Specific points you want covered
The tone (professional, casual, humorous, academic)
Avoid 
Don't put special characters or emojis.
You can write your prompt in any language, and Golpo will generate the video in that language (for creator and above plans). Language option is available from the creator plan and above. For Free and Starter plans, whatever language you provide the prompt in, it'll generate the video in English.
The Attach Button
Below the prompt text box, there is an Attach button. Click it to upload documents — PDFs, Word files, or other text documents. Golpo reads the document, extracts the content, and uses it as the basis for your video. This is powerful for turning existing company documents, research papers, product manuals, or lesson plans into videos without rewriting anything.
You can combine a document attachment with a text prompt. For example, attach your product brochure and add a prompt like "Create a two-minute overview of this product focused on the pricing and key features, targeting small business owners."
Available on: All plans
The Two Toggles: Own Narration and Script Mode
In the top-right corner of the input area, there are two toggles that fundamentally change how Golpo creates your video.
Own Narration
When Own Narration is OFF (the default), Golpo generates the narration for you using AI voices. You provide the topic, and Golpo writes the script and speaks it. A detailed tutorial is available here: https://video.golpoai.com/guide/golpo-ai-own-narration-guide 
When Own Narration is ON , the prompt text box disappears and is replaced with audio and video input options:
Upload Audio or Video — drag and drop or click to select a common audio or video format. For video uploads, choose Audio in Video to use only the audio track or Picture in Picture to keep the presenter visible beside the generated content.
The uploaded video picture is removed only when Audio in Video is selected. Picture in Picture preserves the presenter video and displays it in the chosen overlay or split-screen layout.
Record Audio — record narration directly from your microphone.
Record Webcam — capture camera and audio, then choose Audio in Video or Picture in Picture for the final result.
Golpo uses the supplied audio as the narration instead of generating an AI voice, transcribes the recording, and creates visuals that match it. The recording determines the duration and spoken language, so the normal duration, AI voice, and Voice Instructions controls do not apply in Own Narration mode.
For an uploaded or webcam-recorded video, open the Picture-in-Picture guide to compare round-overlay and split-screen layouts. Before generation, certify that you own the recording or have explicit permission to use it.
Use this when you want to use your own voice, a branded AI voice from ElevenLabs or similar services, an existing recording like a Zoom call or podcast, or when you want precise word-for-word control over the narration.
Available on: Business, Scale, and Enterprise subscription plans. Confirm current availability on the live pricing page .
Script Mode
When Script Mode is ON , the text you supply becomes the narration. Instead of describing a topic and letting Golpo research and draft the script, paste only the words that should be spoken; put visual direction in Video Instructions.
This is essential when:
Compliance or legal requires specific approved language
You have already written a script and want it read exactly
You need total control over what is said without AI interpretation
Access: Edit script before creating video is the separate checkbox that activates the pre-render review step. See the Prompt Mode vs Script Mode guide .
Video Style: Golpo Canvas vs. Golpo Sketch
Below the input area, you choose between two video generation engines:
Golpo Canvas — Produces scene-based animations in 11 named visual styles. Ten established style families offer Compact and Expanded profiles. Compact combines more components within each scene so viewers can see more of the explanation together and retain the relationships between its ideas. Expanded distributes those components across more scenes, giving each component more visual space and revealing the explanation in smaller steps. The earlier profiles are now named Expanded. The new Notebook style is Compact-only. Open the all Canvas styles guide or watch every Compact/Expanded pair plus Notebook .
Custom Style: Business plans and above can create a reusable Canvas style from an image, a video, or a written description. See the Custom Style guide .

Golpo Sketch — Produces hand-drawn whiteboard illustrations with a refined, modern look. Sub-styles include Classic, Improved (Beta), Dry Erase, Professional Clean, Crayon, and Formal. Classic B&W is available on all plans; color videos are only available on the Growth plan and above. Formal style is only available for business plan and above.

Each option has a Preview button next to it so you can see examples of what that style looks like before committing.
Plan availability for styles: 
Free, Starter, and Creator — Golpo Sketch: Classic (B&W) only. Golpo Canvas: Chalkboard B&W only
Growth ($199.99/month) — Golpo Sketch: Classic and Improved (Beta), Dry Erase, Professional Clean, Crayon (Color). Golpo Canvas style access as listed on the current pricing page, with Compact and Expanded profiles where available and the new Compact-only Notebook style
Business, Scale, and Enterprise — all included styles, including Formal and pen-in-hand options; Business and above also support Custom Style
User Videos and Images
Below the style selector, you will see a dashed-border area with two options:
User created videos and images to be inserted in the video (Beta) — upload your own photos, product images, screenshots, or short video clips. Golpo weaves them into the generated video alongside its own illustrations. You can choose whether each image appears as a static still or as an animated illustration. A detailed tutorial is available here: https://video.golpoai.com/guide/how-to-insert-images-in-golpo-ai-videos-step-by-step 
Record screen (with audio) (Beta) — capture a screen recording of your desktop. This is great for product walkthroughs and software demos. Golpo takes your recording and inserts it as clips in the final video
There is also a How to Use? link that opens instructions for this feature.
Available on: Business, Scale, and Enterprise subscription plans.
Audio Only
The Audio Only checkbox creates audio content without generating any video. This produces a podcast-style spoken narration based on your prompt — no visuals, just audio. It uses only 10% of your video credits, making it a very efficient way to produce spoken content.
Use this for:
Podcast episodes generated from your topic or document
Audio summaries of written content
Voiceover tracks you will use elsewhere
Available on: Business, Scale, and Enterprise subscription plans.
Voice Instructions
The text field labeled "Optional: Add voice instructions (e.g., 'Talk in a french accent', 'use an enthusiastic tone', 'talk like a professor')..." lets you control how the AI narrator speaks.
This does not change the words — it changes the delivery. You can request:
A specific accent or speaking style
An enthusiastic, calm, serious, or conversational tone
A particular persona like a professor, news anchor, or storyteller
Pacing preferences like speaking slowly or energetically
Available on: Creator ($99.99/month) and above

Video Instructions
The text field labeled "Optional: Add video instructions (e.g., 'show more graphs and charts', 'include more stock footage', 'use cinematic transitions')..." guides how Golpo generates the visuals.
This is separate from the prompt itself. While the prompt describes what the video is about, video instructions describe how it should look:
"Use blue and orange colors only" — for branding
Use "Times New Roman Font" - specify which fonts the texts appear in on visuals
'Absolutely no text in the video" - to avoid texts showing up in the visuals
"Use cinematic transitions" — for a more polished feel
"Keep illustrations simple and minimal" — for a clean look
"Show more people and faces" — for human-centric topics
Available on: Business and above (appears as an add-on on Growth)

Edit Script Before Creating Video
The Edit script before creating video checkbox tells Golpo to generate the script first and pause so you can review and edit it before any video is generated. The description reads: "Generate and review the script first, then create the video." 

This is extremely useful when you want to:
Review and adjust the AI-generated script before committing credits
Fix specific wording, add details, or remove sections
Ensure accuracy for technical or sensitive topics
See exactly what the video will say before it is produced
Without this checked, Golpo generates the script and video in one step. With it checked, you get a two-step process: script review first, then video generation.
This checkbox is the review control, while Script Mode tells Golpo that the supplied text is the narration.
Color
The Color checkbox — "Enable color in the generated video" — controls whether your video is produced in full color or in black and white.
Black and white gives a classic whiteboard look. Color adds fills, backgrounds, and chromatic detail to the illustrations. Both look professional — it is a stylistic choice. Color option is only available from the Growth and above packages.
Available on: Growth and above 
The Settings Row: Music, Duration, Voice, Orientation, Language, Style
At the bottom of the page, there is a row of dropdown menus that control the technical details of your video. Here is what each one does.

Music
Adds background music to your video. Options include different moods and genres, or set it to None for narration only. There is a Listen link next to this dropdown so you can preview each music option before selecting. You can also upload your custom music as background music from the business plan onward.

Available on: Basic music options vary by plan. Custom music upload is available on Business plans and above.
Duration
Sets the target length of your video. Options range from short clips to longer content:

Free — 1 minute only
Starter — 30 seconds to 2 minutes
Creator — 15 seconds to 2 minutes
Growth — up to 4 minutes; 8- and 10-minute videos are available as an add-on
Business — up to 10 minutes (beta)
Scale — up to 10 minutes (beta)
Enterprise — up to 15 minutes (beta)
For video generation, 1 credit is 60 seconds of Golpo Canvas/Sketch videos and 30 seconds of Golpo Studio videos. Selecting a 2-minute duration uses 2 credits for Golpo Canvas/Sketch videos or 4 credits for Golpo Studio videos. When using Own Narration, the credit cost is based on the actual length of your uploaded audio rather than a selected duration. This article describes the credit deduction rule in details: https://video.golpoai.com/guide/golpo-credit-deduction-guide 
Voice
Selects which AI voice narrates your video. The default is Female 1 . Golpo offers multiple voice options, and there is a Listen link to preview each voice before choosing.

Available AI voices vary by plan. Voice Instructions are included from Creator upward, while Voice Cloning is available on Business plans and above. In Own Narration mode, Golpo uses the supplied recording, so AI voice selection and Voice Instructions do not apply.
Orientation
Controls the video aspect ratio:
Horizontal — Ideal for YouTube, websites, and presentations.
Vertical — Ideal for Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and mobile-first viewing
Available on: Vertical orientation is available on Creator and above. Free and Starter are limited to Horizontal only.
Language
Sets the language for the AI-generated narration. The default is English . Golpo supports multiple languages — you can generate videos in different languages by changing this dropdown. Languages supported are: English, Spanish, French, German, German (Switzerland), Italian, Portuguese, Russian, Chinese, Chinese (Cantonese), Japanese, Korean, Arabic, Hindi, Dutch, Polish, Swedish, Norwegian, Danish, Finnish, Turkish, Greek, Hebrew, Thai, Vietnamese, Indonesian, Malay, Filipino, Czech, Hungarian, Romanian, Bulgarian, Croatian, Serbian, Slovak, Slovenian, Ukrainian, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi, Gujarati, Urdu, Persian, Swahili, Afrikaans.
Available on: Starter has multi-language as an add-on. Creator and above include it as a standard feature.
Display Language
A separate setting from the narration language, Display Language controls the language of any on-screen text that Golpo generates within the video — labels, titles, captions drawn on the whiteboard. This lets you create a video with English (or any) narration but display text in another language, or vice versa. Currently, this option is available for Golpo Canvas videos only.
Available on: Starter has multi-language as an add-on ($40 per month). Creator and above include it as a standard feature.
Style
Selects the sub-style within your chosen video engine (Golpo Canvas or Golpo Sketch). The default is Classic . There is a Preview link to see examples of each style.
For Golpo Sketch, the styles include:
Classic — clean hand-drawn whiteboard look
Classic with words — the Classic look with a few large key words and labels rendered into each scene
Improved (Beta) — refined version of Classic with better illustration quality
Dry Erase — mimics a dry-erase marker on a whiteboard
Professional Clean — polished, corporate-ready illustrations
Crayon — playful, hand-colored crayon look
Formal — refined, serious tone for business and academic content
Classic with words comparison: both players below use the same public Golpo source and the exact same front cover, so you can focus on the color treatment without a thumbnail or composition change.

For Golpo Canvas, the 11 named styles are Chalkboard White on Black, Chalkboard Black on White, Chalkboard Color, Whiteboard, Modern Minimal, Playful, Technical, Editorial, Sharpie, Illustration, and Notebook. The ten pre-Notebook style families offer Compact and Expanded profiles. Pacing
Controls how quickly visuals appear and transition on screen. The default is Fast for Sketch and Normal for Canvas. There is a Preview link to compare pacing options.
Slower pacing gives the viewer more time to absorb each illustration. Faster pacing keeps energy high and is better for shorter, punchier videos. Choose based on your audience and content density.
Logo
The Logo section lets you add your own logo to the generated video. Click Add to upload a logo image. There is also a dropdown with TL (Top Left), BL (Bottom Left), TR (Top Right), and BR (Bottom Right) to ascertain where the logo needs to be placed in the video.
Adding a logo to every video you create — useful for companies, agencies, and content creators who want consistent branding.
Available on: Growth has a custom logo as an add-on. Business and Scale include it as a standard feature.
The Generate Video Button
At the bottom-right of the page is the Generate Video button. Once you have configured everything, click it to start generation.
Before generation starts, you will see an estimated generation time — typically 10 to 15 minutes , though this varies based on your prompt, selected duration, and current server load.
You'll be presented with this screen when you hit the Generate Video for you to opt in or opt out of showing the video on Golpo AI video gallery https://video.golpoai.com/gallery - you can change this visibility setting later in the library - just click on the eye in the top right corner of each video in library to toggle visibility in the public gallery.

After clicking Generate Video, see the progress till about 2%, and you can close the page and come back later. An email notification will come to you when the video is ready. Your video will appear in the Library when it is ready.

Downloading Your Video
To download a video, find it in the Library and click the download icon (the downward arrow). The video downloads as an MP4 file that you can use anywhere — upload to YouTube, embed on your website, include in presentations, or send directly to clients.
The downloaded file includes the full video with all visuals, narration, and music baked in. It is ready to publish as-is or import into a video editor for further customization.
Editing Your Video
Golpo offers frame-by-frame editing that lets you modify the generated video after creation. Click the edit icon (pen icon) on any video card in the Library to open the editor.
In the editor, you can:
Edit visuals frame by frame — change illustrations, adjust drawings, modify what appears on screen for any individual frame
Add or replace images — insert your own images into specific frames or replace Golpo-generated illustrations with your own
Upload reference images — provide a reference image and Golpo regenerates the frame to match it (Golpo Sketch only)
Adjust text — modify any on-screen text within frames
Note that audio cannot be edited through the frame editor. If you need to change the narration, you will need to regenerate the video.
A detailed guide on how editing works is here: https://video.golpoai.com/guide/golpo-video-editing-guide

The Typical Workflow: Start to Finish
To bring everything together, here is the standard workflow from start to finish:
Start: click Create New Video.
Choose the input workflow: write a prompt, enable Script Mode for an exact script, or enable Own Narration to upload or record audio/video.
Choose the engine: Golpo Canvas or Golpo Sketch.
Add optional media: attach source documents, insert your own images/videos, or record the screen when those controls apply.
Add instructions: Voice Instructions apply to AI narration; Video Instructions can direct the visual treatment. Voice Instructions do not apply in Own Narration mode.
Review the script: enable Edit script before creating video when you want approval before generation.
Configure applicable settings: music, orientation, visual style, pacing, color, and logo. For AI narration, also choose duration, voice, and language. Own Narration derives duration and spoken language from the recording.
For video narration: choose Audio in Video or Picture in Picture and configure its layout.
Confirm rights: when using Own Narration, certify that you own the recording or have explicit permission to use it.
Generate: click Generate Video and use the estimate displayed in the product; actual timing varies.
Manage the result: open Library to play, download, edit, compare versions, or share the video.
That is the entire platform. Every control on the page maps to a specific aspect of your video — what it says, how it sounds, how it looks, and what format it is delivered in. Start with a simple prompt and the default settings, watch the result, and then experiment with the controls one at a time to dial in exactly the output you want.
