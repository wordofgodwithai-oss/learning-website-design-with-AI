Features & Editing Introducing Golpo Avatar: Turn a Selfie into a Talking AI Presenter
Golpo now supports talking AI avatars. Create a custom presenter from a selfie or choose a stock Golpo Avatar, explore layouts, and watch nine examples.
Liam Park • October 1, 2026 • 11 min read 

Golpo now supports talking AI avatars . Upload a portrait or selfie to create your own presenter, or choose a ready-to-use stock avatar from Golpo. Then bring that presenter into a video alongside the visuals that explain your story.

Your avatar can sit beside a whiteboard explanation, appear in a round overlay, take the full screen, or share a vertical frame with your content. You choose the presentation—or let Auto choose among your approved layouts for each scene.

Combine your presenter with Golpo, the world's best AI explainer video maker, for a winning combination. Your presenter brings a face to the story, while Golpo's animated visuals help explain the idea. Together, they give you a personal way to teach, introduce a product, share a founder update, or walk an audience through something new.

Two ways to choose your presenter

Start with a stock Golpo Avatar

Open Talking AI Avatars and choose Golpo Avatars . The library shown here contains 20 presenters ready for video generation, with options such as Maya, Arjun, Sofia, and Daniel.

A stock avatar is a useful starting point for a lesson, product explanation, onboarding video, or business update when you want a presenter without uploading a portrait. For the library screenshots, step-by-step setup, and all 20 presenter examples, see the Golpo Stock Avatars guide .

Create a custom avatar from your image

Want to put yourself in the video? Choose Create Avatar and upload a portrait or selfie. Golpo lets you frame the image, review its presentation, name the avatar, and save it for reuse in My Avatars .

This is a useful way to give a founder update, teach a lesson, or introduce a product with your own on-screen identity.

How to create an avatar from a selfie

1. Upload and frame a clear portrait

In Talking AI Avatars → Create Avatar , drag your image into the upload area or click to browse. The upload screen accepts PNG, JPEG, or WebP files up to 10 MB .

Use a photo with one person facing the camera, with the full head, chin, and shoulders visible. Even lighting, a simple background, and a sharp image help the animation. Avoid group photos, hands covering the face, dark sunglasses, heavy shadows, blur, and cropped hair.

Frame the portrait, then choose Continue to review .

Start with a clear portrait. The creation screen says your portrait and saved avatar stay in your personal library by default. 

2. Choose Natural or Polished

The review screen offers two presenter styles:

Natural avatar: uses your original portrait and approved framing. It is ready immediately and has no avatar creation credit charge .

Polished avatar: creates an AI-styled portrait designed to retain your recognizable appearance. It costs 1 credit after successful creation .

3. Review layouts, name your avatar, and save

Preview the layouts you want to use: Side by side , Top and bottom , Round overlay , and Full screen . Approve the layouts that look correct, adjust the portrait if needed, enter an avatar name, and complete the image-use confirmation before saving.

The review screenshot below shows this stage with another portrait. The nine gallery videos later in this guide use Shraman's black T-shirt selfie shown in the gallery introduction.

Review the presenter style and layout previews, name the avatar, and confirm image use before saving. 

4. Select the avatar for your video

Enable Talking AI Avatar in your video settings. Choose a saved custom avatar from Mine / My Avatars , select a stock presenter from Golpo / Golpo Avatars , or use Create new .

Choose your presentation settings and check the live preview before continuing with video generation. The selected avatar card and credit-rate panel show the applicable rate.

Choose how your avatar shares the screen

The avatar adds a presenter to your explanation. The layout determines how much space that presenter and your supporting visuals receive.

Adjust presenter size and placement

For the landscape split shown in the settings screenshot, the presenter can use 25%, 30%, 35%, 40%, or 50% of the width , with placement on the left or right .

Use a 50/50 split when you want the presenter and content to have equal space. A smaller presenter leaves more room for diagrams, labels, and visual explanations. The gallery includes both 50/50 and 35% presenter examples, including vertical videos.

Let Auto adapt the presentation

Auto chooses among the approved layouts that fit each scene. When at least two visible layouts are compatible, it uses at least two. It may briefly hide the avatar to keep important content unobstructed.

The Preview Auto layouts tabs let you inspect appearances such as Side by side, Round overlay, and Full screen. Switching those preview tabs does not change the generation setting: it still uses Auto.

Frequently asked questions

Can I create an avatar from a selfie?

Yes. Upload a clear portrait or selfie in Create Avatar , frame it, review and approve suitable layouts, name it, and save it to your library.

Do I need to upload my own photo?

No. Choose a ready-to-use presenter from Golpo Avatars .

What is the difference between Natural and Polished?

Natural uses your original portrait and approved framing, with no creation credit charge. Polished creates an AI-styled portrait designed to keep your recognizable appearance and uses 1 credit after successful creation.

Can I reuse my custom avatar?

Yes. Save it in My Avatars , then select it for another video. You do not need to start from the upload screen each time.

Does uploading a selfie clone my voice?

A selfie supplies the avatar's appearance. Set the narration voice separately. For Golpo's voice workflow, see how to clone your voice for AI video .

Can I make vertical Avatar videos?

Yes. The gallery includes vertical split layouts and a vertical Auto example. Preview Top and bottom when reviewing your avatar's portrait 9:16 presentation.

Will the avatar appear throughout an Auto video?

Auto may briefly hide the avatar to keep important content unobstructed. It chooses among the layouts you approved that fit the scene.

Where does my uploaded portrait go?

The creation screen states that your portrait and saved avatar stay in your personal library by default.
