How to Edit Videos on Golpo AI: Frame-by-Frame Editing Guide

Golpo doesn't just generate videos — it lets you edit them frame by frame after generation. This is one of the features that separates Golpo from most AI video tools, where what you get is what you get.

In this guide, I'll cover how the editing feature works, what you can and can't change, how credits are handled, and which plans include it.

Where to Find the Edit Button

Once your video is generated, head to your Library page . Under each video, you'll see a small pen icon — that's the Edit button. Click it to open the frame-by-frame editor.

What You Can Edit

1. Edit Visuals Frame by Frame

The editor lets you go through your video one frame at a time and make targeted changes to the visuals. You're not re-generating the whole video — you're surgically fixing individual frames.

2. Add or Replace Images and Text

You can add new images or words/letters to any frame, or replace existing ones . This is useful when:

The AI got a visual wrong and you want to swap it out

You want to insert your own branding or diagram

A word or label in a frame needs correcting

3. Upload Reference Images (Golpo Sketch Only)

For Golpo Sketch videos specifically, you can upload a reference image and have it placed into a frame — either as an addition alongside existing visuals, or as a replacement for another image. This gives you precise control over the visual content without needing to regenerate.

4. Edit Audio

Audio editing is now available in Golpo, alongside frame-by-frame visual editing.

How Credits Work for Editing

This is important to understand before you start:

The first edited version is free across sessions until finalized. Later versions deduct credits equal to the video length: 1 minute costs 1 credit; 2 minutes costs 2 credits.

Finalizing that version does not cost any additional credits. You can tweak as many frames as you want within that version, across sessions, without extra charges.

If you start another version after finalizing, credits are deducted when you begin the new version.

Start the first edited version of a 1-min video: 0 credits

Edit frames within that version, across sessions: No additional cost

Finalize the version: No additional cost

Start a new version after finalizing: 1 credit

The first edited version is free; later versions cost the same credits as generating the video.

Which Plans Include Editing?

Editing is included on all paid plans :

Starter and Creator: Included 

Growth: Included

Business and above: Included
