Where it works Built into the places you already work

🎫 In Jira

Issue panel
Generate from any issue — title, description, and attachments auto-fill the prompt.

Project page (bulk)
Filter and select many issues, set defaults, and batch-generate. Admins can auto-generate on new tickets.

Board sprint summary
Generate a sprint or board-overview video; schedule daily, on sprint close, or on custom days.

Admin settings
Site admins connect, change, or disconnect the shared API key.

📄 In Confluence

Page byline
Generate from any page — title, content, labels, and attachments are read automatically.

Context menu
Highlight text → right-click → generate a quick video from just that selection.

Space page (bulk)
List every page in a space, filter, set defaults, and batch-generate.

Macro embed
Type /golpo to embed a generated video inline on any page.

What you can do Available in both apps

🎬 One-click generation
Turn an issue, sprint, or page into a finished narrated video. It runs on Golpo servers (~3–35 min) — close the panel and come back.

🎙️ AI voice narration
Natural narration in 4 voices (female & male) with free-text voice instructions.

🌍 45+ languages
English, Spanish, French, German, Japanese, Hindi, Arabic, Bengali, and many more.

🎨 19 art styles
10 Canvas styles + 9 Sketch variants — whiteboard, chalkboard, modern minimal, technical, crayon, and more.

📦 Bulk generation
Generate across a whole Jira project or Confluence space, with filters and per-item settings.

📎 Rich attachments
Add images, video, documents (PDF, DOCX, PPTX, XLSX…), and audio — insert into the video or use as AI reference.

🎵 8 music tracks
Engaging, Hyper, Inspirational, Documentary, Jazz, Lo-fi, Dramatic — or none.

⚙️ Full control
Duration (15s–10 min), pacing, orientation (16:9 / 9:16), pen style, color, and video instructions.

Frequently asked questions
Do I need an API key to get started? + No — every install includes a free starter pool for short videos (30s–1 min). Add an API key for longer videos and ongoing use.
How long does video generation take? + About 3–35 minutes depending on duration. It runs on Golpo AI servers, so you can close the panel and come back later.
How many languages, voices, and styles are there? + 45+ languages, 4 narration voices, and 19 art styles (10 Canvas + 9 Sketch).
What can I attach to a video? + Images (JPG, PNG, GIF, WEBP), video (MP4, MOV, …), documents (PDF, DOCX, PPTX, XLSX, …), and audio (MP3, WAV, …).
Who configures the API key? + A site administrator connects it once in the app's settings; after that, everyone on the site can generate without their own key.
