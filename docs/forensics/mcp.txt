Tools Everything ChatGPT can do with Golpo 
Once connected, ChatGPT can use these 6 tools on your Golpo account.

Generate video
generate_video
Turn a prompt, script, or uploaded file into a Golpo video or podcast. Pick the length, style, language, voice, and pacing.

Upload file
upload_file
Upload a public HTTPS file, such as a voice clip, logo, or reference image, and use it in a video.

List videos
list_videos
Browse your most recent Golpo videos with a share link for each one.

Get video
get_video
Open a single video by ID to get its share page, and optionally its script.

Check status
check_status
Check whether a video that is still generating has finished.

Connector status
status
Confirm the connector is signed in and which server version you are connected to.
